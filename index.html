Optical metrology · Experimental mechanics

Chuanguo Xiong

Optical measurement and mechanical characterization

I work at the intersection of optical measurement, experimental mechanics, and computer vision. My research focuses on understanding how structures deform, using digital image correlation (DIC), thermography, and image analysis.

I received my M.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering from Huazhong University of Science and Technology in 2024. My background also includes embedded software development for medical mechatronic systems.

Research

My research connects optical experiments with computational methods to measure deformation and study structural response.

01 Full-field measurement

DIC and thermography

Combined stereo DIC and thermography to capture deformation and thermal information in packaged structures under thermal loading. The work brings mechanical and thermal measurements into a common experimental framework.

  • Stereo DIC
  • Thermography
  • Camera calibration
Related publication ↗
02 Image analysis

Automated ROI extraction

Developed attention-enhanced U-Net++ methods for automated region-of-interest extraction from heterogeneous surfaces. The DIC measurement framework was evaluated against Moiré interferometry.

  • U-Net++
  • PyTorch
  • Projected speckle DIC
Related publication ↗
03 Computational mechanics

MEMS gyroscope simulation

Investigated tuning fork gyroscope microstructures using modal and harmonic response analyses in Ansys. Applied the Taguchi method to study how design parameters affect vibration transmission and sensitivity.

  • Finite element analysis
  • MEMS dynamics
  • Design optimization
